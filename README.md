# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard  

This project features an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes coffee shop sales data.  
It offers valuable insights into **revenue performance**, **customer preferences**, and **product trends**, helping business owners make informed, data-driven decisions to improve overall sales and profitability.  

---

## 🎯 Project Objective  

The primary objective of this project is to analyze **daily coffee shop sales** data and uncover meaningful insights that drive better business decisions.  
This includes identifying the **key factors influencing total revenue**, determining **top-selling products**, understanding **customer behavior patterns**, and evaluating **sales performance across different time periods and product categories**.  

By using **Power BI**, **Excel**, and **SQL**, this dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance — enabling data-driven strategies to boost sales, enhance customer satisfaction, and optimize inventory management.  

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies Used  
- **Power BI** – Dashboard design and visualization  
- **SQL** – Data cleaning and querying  
- **Excel** – Data preparation and support

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)  

The dashboard focuses on key business metrics that provide a clear view of overall performance and customer behavior:

- ☕ **Total Sales Revenue** – Measures the total income generated from all sales.  
- 🛍️ **Average Order Value** – Calculates the average amount spent per customer order.  
- 📦 **Total Quantity Sold** – Tracks the total number of items sold during a selected period.  
- 🧁 **Sales by Product Category** – Analyzes which product categories contribute most to revenue.  
- 🕒 **Peak Sales Hours** – Identifies the busiest hours of the day for improved staffing and inventory planning.  

---

## 🔍 Project Insights  

- ☕ **Top Products:** Identified best-selling coffee items that contribute the most to total revenue.  
- ⏰ **Peak Hours:** Found that morning hours (8 AM–11 AM) record the highest customer traffic and sales volume.  
- ❄️ **Seasonal Trends:** Sales of cold beverages rise significantly during summer months, showing strong seasonal demand.  
- 💳 **Customer Loyalty:** Noted that loyalty programs have a positive correlation with repeat purchases and higher average order values.  
- 📈 **Growth Opportunities:** Insights suggest focusing on popular beverage categories and morning-hour promotions to maximize revenue.
